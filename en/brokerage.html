1. Holdership verification

1.1 Before marketing or leasing the Block, Hexxa checks holdership in the WHOIS/RDAP records of the regional internet registry (the "RIR") and confirms that the acceptance came from an e-mail address on the registered organization's domain or from a contact registered with the RIR. Hexxa may request additional documents and decline the engagement if verification is not completed.

1.2 The Holder represents that: (a) it is the legitimate holder of the Block with the RIR, or is authorized to represent it; (b) the Block is free of liens, encumbrances, litigation, disputes or conflicting usage agreements; (c) neither it nor its shareholders or directors are targets of UN, US (OFAC) or European Union sanctions; (d) the information provided is true.

2. Brokered sale

2.1 Hexxa markets the Block to verified buyers at the net price requested by the Holder. Hexxa does not close any deal on the Holder's behalf: every offer is presented to the Holder, who decides.

2.2 Success fee. The Holder (seller) pays Hexxa 5% of the total sale price, only if the transfer at the RIR is completed with a buyer introduced by Hexxa. The fee is also due if a sale to a buyer introduced by Hexxa is completed within 12 months of the introduction, even if negotiated directly between the parties. The fee falls due upon completion of the transfer and is paid within 5 business days, or withheld by the escrow agent, where one is used.

2.3 Payment. The price is paid between buyer and seller through an escrow service (for example, Escrow.com) or as the parties agree in writing. Hexxa does not receive or hold the sale price, unless agreed in writing.

2.4 Transfer. Each party handles its own steps at the RIR: documentation, fees and transfer policy requirements. Hexxa provides guidance on the process, without guaranteeing RIR approval.

2.5 Hexxa is not a party to the sale agreement and is not liable for the obligations of the buyer or the seller.

3. Managed lease

3.1 The Holder authorizes Hexxa, as its agent, to sublease the Block, in whole or in /24 parts, to customers verified by Hexxa, under Hexxa's IPv4 Lease Terms of Service and Acceptable Use Policy.

3.2 Hexxa is responsible for customer verification (KYC), billing, enforcement of the Acceptable Use Policy and handling of abuse reports concerning the Block. Hexxa informs the Holder of each customer's ASN and company name.

3.3 Revenue share. The Holder receives 80% of the net monthly fee actually received by Hexxa from each customer for the use of the Block. The net monthly fee is the amount received, less taxes on revenue, payment processing fees and chargebacks. Payouts are made monthly, by the 15th day of the month following receipt, to the account designated by the Holder, with a statement. Amounts not received from customers do not generate a payout.

3.4 LOA and ROA. The Holder authorizes Hexxa, as its agent, to: (a) issue, on the Holder's behalf, the Letter of Authorization (LOA) for the ASN of each customer approved by Hexxa; and (b) request by e-mail the creation and removal of the ROA and routing objects (IRR) for that ASN, which the Holder carries out within 2 business days of the request. Alternatively, the Holder may give Hexxa RIR API access or a technical contact with those rights, so that Hexxa can do it. When a customer leaves, the LOA is revoked and the records are removed within the same period.

3.5 While any part of the Block is leased to a Hexxa customer, the Holder does not announce it, lease it to third parties or transfer it. If the Holder decides to sell the Block, it notifies Hexxa 60 days in advance, and current leases are honored until the end of the billing cycle already contracted by the customer.

3.6 Term. The managed lease has a minimum term of 6 months from acceptance. After that, either party may end it with 60 days' notice by e-mail. Leases in progress at the end of the notice period continue until the end of the billing cycle already contracted by the customer, with normal payouts.

3.7 Either party may terminate immediately in case of a false representation, a material breach not remedied within 10 days of notice, sanctions, an RIR or authority order, or loss of holdership of the Block.
