1. Who we are

1.1 HEXXA LTDA, CNPJ 63.048.597/0001-64, Rua Pais Leme, 215, conj. 1713, Pinheiros, São Paulo/SP, CEP 05424-150, Brazil ("Hexxa"), is the controller of the personal data described in this Policy, under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018, "LGPD").

2. What data we process and why

2.1 Customers and prospective customers: name, position, e-mail and phone of representatives and contacts (technical, billing and abuse), company registration data and technical data (ASN, prefixes, declared use). Purposes: sending quotes, customer verification (KYC), performing the agreement, billing, legal compliance and fraud and abuse prevention. Legal bases: pre-contractual steps and performance of contract, legal obligation, exercise of rights and legitimate interest (LGPD art. 7, II, V, VI and IX).

2.2 Business contacts: professional data from public sources (RIR WHOIS/RDAP records, PeeringDB, company websites and public registries), used to offer our services to companies in the sector. Legal basis: legitimate interest (art. 7, IX). Business contact by e-mail and phone may be made with the help of automated systems, including an AI assistant.

2.3 Payments: payment data is processed directly by Stripe. Hexxa does not store card data.

3. Sharing

3.1 We share data only as needed with: the Supplier and Holder of the Block and the regional internet registry (including publication of the abuse contact in WHOIS/RDAP); the payment processor (Stripe); infrastructure providers (cloud hosting, e-mail, telephony and AI processing); KYC and sanctions-screening providers; and authorities, when required by law.

3.2 Some of these providers are located outside Brazil. International transfers follow the LGPD rules (art. 33).

4. Retention

4.1 Customer data: during the agreement and for 5 years after termination, unless a longer legal period applies. Business contacts (item 2.2): until the data subject objects, or up to 2 years without interaction; do-not-contact requests are kept so that no further contact is made.

5. Your rights

5.1 Data subjects may request confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information on sharing, review of automated decisions, and may object to processing (LGPD art. 18), through comercial@hexxacrm.com. To stop receiving business contacts, simply reply to any e-mail asking to be removed, or say so during the call.

5.2 Data subjects may also lodge a complaint with the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

6. Security and changes

6.1 We apply reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect data, such as access control and storing credentials in a secrets vault. This Policy may be updated; the current version is always on this page.
